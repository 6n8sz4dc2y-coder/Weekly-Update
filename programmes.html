Admin Update

This hub reads workbooks uploaded to GitHub, each with an "Export" sheet: four VCF exports (a Centre or CDA column, followed by pillar groups of Actual / Target / SvO columns) for Centre and CDA level, each for Year to Date and Q3; one Group Trade Parts export (a flat quarterly table, Group-level); a Group Trade Parts CDA breakdown (one file per CDA - select all of them at once, the CDA name is read automatically from each file); one Group Trade Parts Lexus export (same shape, reported separately from the geographic CDAs); and two WRR exports (a flat per-centre table, Q3 and Year to Date). Replace whichever file(s) changed each period, commit, and refresh the dashboard. The VCF parser is dynamic, so new or changed pillars in any export are picked up automatically.
