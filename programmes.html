Admin Update

This hub reads up to five workbooks uploaded to GitHub, each with an "Export" sheet: four VCF exports (a Centre or CDA column, followed by pillar groups of Actual / Target / SvO columns) for Centre and CDA level, each for Year to Date and Q3; plus one Group Trade Parts export (a flat quarterly table, Group-level only). Replace whichever file(s) changed each period, commit, and refresh the dashboard. The VCF parser is dynamic, so new or changed pillars in any export are picked up automatically.
